Welcome to OpsPilot! This guide explains what this application does, 
the problems it solves, and how to use each of its features in simple, 
human-friendly language. No programming knowledge required!

---

1. THE PROBLEM OPSPILOT SOLVES
========================================================================
In modern business teams, important decisions and tasks are scattered 
everywhere: in Slack messages, Zoom call transcripts, email threads, and 
huddled discussions. 

Keeping track of who promised to do what is a major headache:
- Key action items get lost in long meeting notes.
- Updating project trackers (like Jira or Trello) is tedious and manual.
- Managers struggle to see who is overloaded and which tasks are blocked.

OpsPilot is your AI-Native Operational Intelligence Assistant. It listens 
to your unstructured team conversations, extracts actionable checklists 
using Artificial Intelligence, tracks progress in visual dashboard grids, 
safeguards team workloads, and syncs tasks directly to project trackers.

---

2. STEP-BY-STEP FUNCTIONALITY GUIDE
========================================================================

Follow this step-by-step guide to navigate and make the most of OpsPilot:

STEP 1: The Security Gateway (Login & Registration)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before accessing the hub, you must authenticate through the gateway.
1. Enter your Identity Email and Access Password.
2. If you are new, click "Register" to create an account:
   - Provide your Full Name and select your Workspace Role:
     * Team Collaborator: Standard team member.
     * Operations Coordinator: Manages team deliverables.
     * System Administrator: Full system oversight.
3. Click "Establish Connection" to access the main hub.

STEP 2: Live Monitoring (The Strategic Dashboard)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once logged in, you enter the main Dashboard—a visual control panel 
presenting the real-time health of your operations:
1. "Strategic Overview": Shows the general system sync status.
2. "Active AI Insights": A smart message board where the AI highlights 
   immediate risks (e.g., critical bottlenecks or workload imbalances).
3. "Bento Grid Cards":
   - "Active Sprints": Shows the number of concurrent projects.
   - "AI Streams": A live sync pulse showing the AI is processing data.
   - "Bottlenecks": A red warning gauge highlighting stalled pipelines.
4. "Team Operations": A list of active, high-priority tasks and who is 
   assigned to lead them.
5. "System Logs": A real-time timeline of background technical events.

STEP 3: Team Sprints (Kanban Deliverables)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Team Sprints" in the navigation drawer to manage tasks visually:
1. "Workspace Selector": Choose which team board you want to view from 
   the dropdown at the top.
2. "Kanban Columns": Tasks are categorized into four columns:
   - "To Do" (Not started)
   - "In Progress" (Active)
   - "Blocked" (Stalled)
   - "Completed" (Resolved)
3. "Managing Cards":
   - Click the "Arrow" button on a card to advance it to the next stage.
   - If a task gets stuck, click "Block Task" to flag it in red. Click 
     "Unblock" once resolved.
   - Delete tasks by clicking the small trash bin icon (Admin only).
4. "Adding Tasks & Collaborators":
   - Click "Add Task" to input a Title, description, priority, deadline, 
     and assign it to a team member.
   - Click "Members" to view active team collaborators or invite new ones.

STEP 4: AI Operations (Your Smart Workspace)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the core of the platform—a split-screen AI workspace:
1. "Left Column (Semantic Registers)":
   - Ingest new memory into the AI by typing plain text or notes in the 
     form and clicking "Ingest Log to Memory."
   - Or drag and drop text (.txt) files directly into the file uploader.
   - The AI instantly indexes these files for RAG search capability.
2. "Right Column (AI Chat Console)":
   - Type questions in the input bar at the bottom, or click the quick 
     shortcut pills (e.g. "Show overdue tasks").
   - Ask questions like: "What was discussed in the sync?" or "Are 
     there any blockers?"
   - The AI will formulate answers, highlighting the exact sources.
3. "Interactive Checklists":
   - When you ask the AI to extract tasks, it generates an interactive 
     checklist directly inside the chat response.
   - You can check/uncheck these items as they get completed.
   - Click "Sync to Jira" at the bottom of the checklist. The AI will 
     simulate a sync and lock the tasks inside your project management system!

STEP 5: Alerts & Reports (Risk Control)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Alerts & Reports" to forecast risks and resolve bottlenecks:
1. "Timeline Risks": Flags tasks that are overdue or close to deadline.
2. "Execution Velocity Speedometer": A beautiful circular dial showing 
   the percentage of tasks resolved. Your target optimal velocity is 65%.
3. "Capacity Balance Indices": Checks team workloads:
   - If a member is managing too many complex tasks, the system triggers 
     a "Critical Overload" warning to alert the coordinator.
4. "Active Pipeline Blockers & Assign AI Agent":
   - Blocked tasks are listed with their overdue duration.
   - Click "Assign AI Agent" next to any blocker. The system spins an 
     automated processing wheel and assigns a specialized AI agent to 
     mitigate the bottleneck. Once completed, the badge glows green.

STEP 6: Personalization & Comfort (Dark Mode & Profiles)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
OpsPilot is designed to look premium and feel comfortable to work in:
1. "Sun/Moon Toggle": Click the Sun/Moon icon in the top header bar 
   (or bottom sidebar) to switch instantly between light and dark modes:
   - Light mode is a warm, matte-paper Bisque that reduces eye strain.
   - Dark mode is a deep warm Espresso chocolate theme, perfect for late 
     night operations.
2. "Edit Operator Profile": Click the small pencil/edit icon next to 
   your name in the sidebar:
   - Change your display name or role.
   - Choose a premium cinematic executive avatar from our curated list.
   - Click "Save Changes" to update your appearance instantly. Your 
     preferences are saved locally and persist on page reloads!
